Who We Are

Building pathways to dignity, opportunity and hope.

LEAP Aid International is a humanitarian and community development organisation with current operations in South Sudan.

LEAP’s work is guided by education, youth empowerment and humanitarian support. Specific programme activities and future geographic expansion will be shared when confirmed.
