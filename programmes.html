Programmes for possibility.

Three proposed programme areas focused on opportunity, dignity and community wellbeing.

Planned programme areas

Building a thoughtful foundation for future action.

LEAP Aid International is at the founding stage. The programme areas below describe intended priorities and are not currently active programmes. Locations, partnerships, safeguards and delivery plans will be confirmed before implementation.

Planned programme

01 / Education

LEAP Education Initiative

Intended to help address educational barriers for children and learners through practical, community-informed support.

Areas under consideration

  • Educational opportunities and access
  • Scholastic materials
  • Literacy support
  • Mentorship
  • Educational partnerships

02 / Youth

LEAP Youth Empowerment

Intended to support young people as they explore skills, employment, entrepreneurship and meaningful participation in community life.

Areas under consideration

  • Digital skills
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Vocational training
  • Career guidance
  • Youth leadership

03 / Humanitarian response

LEAP Humanitarian Response

Intended to prepare for responsible, timely support to vulnerable families and communities affected by emergencies or displacement.

Areas under consideration

  • Emergency assistance
  • Food and essential supplies
  • Support for displaced communities
  • Assistance to vulnerable families
  • Coordination with appropriate partners
