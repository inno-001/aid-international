Our Purpose

Mission and Vision

Our guiding purpose and the future we hope to help make possible.

A founding direction

As a proposed organisation at the founding stage, LEAP’s mission and vision describe its intended purpose and long-term direction. They are a foundation for future planning, not a claim of current programme operations.

Our Vision

“A world where every individual, regardless of their background or circumstances, has access to opportunities, dignity and the resources necessary to build a better future.”

Our Mission

“To empower vulnerable and underserved communities through education, humanitarian assistance, youth empowerment and sustainable community development initiatives that improve lives and create lasting opportunities.”

Core Values

How we intend to work.

01

Humanity

We recognise the dignity and worth of every person.

02

Integrity

We aim to act honestly, ethically and responsibly.

03

Compassion

We seek to respond with care, respect and empathy.

04

Equality

We promote fairness, inclusion and equal opportunity.

05

Transparency

06

Empowerment

We value people’s agency in shaping their own futures.

07

Sustainability

We consider lasting community wellbeing and resilience.

Strategic Objectives

Turning purpose into considered priorities.

These objectives describe intended areas of focus. They are not a statement that programmes are currently active.

  1. 01

    Promote access to inclusive, quality education and learning opportunities.

  2. 02

    Support young people to build practical skills, confidence and leadership.

  3. 03

    Develop responsible humanitarian support for people affected by vulnerability, displacement or crisis.

  4. 04

    Strengthen community participation and locally informed development approaches.

  5. 05

    Build transparent partnerships and accountable organisational practices.

  6. 06

    Learn from verified evidence to improve relevance, quality and sustainability.
